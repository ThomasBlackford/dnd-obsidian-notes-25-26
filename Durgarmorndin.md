Durgarmorndin is one of the [[5 Great Dragons]]. He resides in the [[Deep North]] of [[Aurius]], and has great control and influence over the continent. Being a Green Dragon, he is naturally cunning and manipulative. He is the cause of the [[Rootrot]], wanting to return the world to its natural state where nature was prosperous and views humanoids as infectious diseases. This philosophy comes directly from a corrupted member of [[The Druidic Order]], the [[Man of Bark]], who has enabled him to have the magical power to cause this devastating plague.
