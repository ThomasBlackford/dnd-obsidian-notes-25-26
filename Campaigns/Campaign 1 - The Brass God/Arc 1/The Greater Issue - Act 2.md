# Session 1 - From the Ashes of Graybend
After the [[Rootrot]] Plaugebeast ravaged the town of [[Graybend]] and killed most of its inhabitants, the party is left with multiple hooks to go off of.

### Rebuilding
Graybend has been destroyed by Rootrot. It is unsure where it came from, as no other towns in the area have been affected like this. The PCs have a choice to make, as there are only a few survivors left. Hoxanad Scouts will attempt to secure the town and turn it into a military outpost, clearing out the few survivors. 

Among the rubble, the PCs can find an encoded journal that references [[Mossden]] and [[Rootrot]] experiments. It is stamped with the [[Caelmithar]] crest.

After this, rumors of goblins can be heard from local merchants and such.
