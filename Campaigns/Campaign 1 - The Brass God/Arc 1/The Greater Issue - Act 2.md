# Session 1 - From the Ashes of Graybend
After the [[Rootrot]] Plaugebeast ravaged the town of [[Graybend]] and killed most of its inhabitants, the party is left with multiple hooks to go off of.

### Rebuilding
Graybend has been destroyed by Rootrot. It is unsure where it came from, as no other towns in the area have been affected like this. The PCs have a choice to make, as there are only a few survivors left. Hoxanad Scouts will attempt to secure the town and turn it into a military outpost, clearing out the few survivors. 

Among the rubble, the PCs can find an encoded journal that references [[Mossden]] and [[Rootrot]] experiments. It is stamped with the [[Caelmithar]] crest. This Journal contains info on some piece of crucial info the Goblins have, and reads as this:

"The trials in Mossden yielded incomplete results. The Rootrot responds erratically to surface exposure and cannot yet be weaponized without a stabilizing agent. Local scholars failed to perceive its true potential — but the goblins of  Krazzah'Kah guard something far older. Our scouts confirm whispers of a relic beneath their city. They possess crucial info we must obtain.”

The Rootrot

*I lost track here but they have ended up in the Rooted City.*

The Rooted City has 3 paths. One to the left, the Forge district. To the right, the Aether Conduit. The central door is the Throne Room.

# The Forge District
The entrance to the collapsed workshop is sealed at first. The party must look around the first layer and navigate various hostile enemies and solve a puzzle.

The puzzle:
A long workshop chamber with **a central brass table** that has **five valve wheels** built into it — each carved with **a dwarven rune**. Pipes lead from the table into the walls, one toward the **main forge door** (currently sealed), and the others into the walls where faint _steam hisses and leaks_.

Puzzle flow:
There are 5 valves, each corresponding to an element.
From right to left:
Fire, water, air, earth, corruption
Only one correct sequence opens the door, turning the wrong one releases a hostile hazard.

Clues:
A mural near the wall shows dwarves forging metal. "A bellows blows into the flames, molten metal quenched in water, hammered upon stone."

A broken slate besides the door has Dwarvish text:
"When corruption seeps, it must be bled last, lest the forge be consumed."

The valves each have symbols on them.

From these, observant players can deduce:
 **Order = Air → Fire → Water → Earth → Corruption**
If they turn them wrong:

- 1st mistake → steam burst (Dex save or 1d4 fire damage)
- 2nd mistake → pressure release triggers **animated forge guardians** (CR 1/2 constructs)
- 3rd → valve system breaks; must repair with a tinker’s check (DC 14)

Other rooms

Storage room: Racks of tools, few workbenches, construct parts, a small schematic. Loot:
- 1–2 potions (Oil of Slipperiness, Alchemist’s Fire, minor healing)
- 30–40 gp worth of scrap metal
- A dwarven schematic of “The Brassheart Engine”

Forge room: A broken forge, piles of rubble with roots in the corner. One forge guardian in this room. No loot.

## ⚙️ **Boss Encounter: The Brassbound Forge-Tender**

**Location:**  
The Forge Chamber — a circular platform of blackened steel suspended over a lake of lava. Thick pipes coil around the edges, hissing steam and belching heat. The central forge sits on a dais, glowing with dull embers. Three large **valves** line the perimeter.

**Trigger:**  
When the PCs activate the first valve, gears grind to life, and molten metal begins to flow. The chamber rumbles as something _massive_ stirs beneath the forge.

---

### **Phase 1 — The Awakening**

> As the forge ignites, the sound of grinding metal echoes through the chamber. The floor quakes, and a massive dwarven automaton rises from the molten pool — a fusion of brass, gears, and twisted rootlike vines pulsing faintly with green light.
> 
> Steam vents open across its armor, and its voice booms in broken Dwarvish:  
> _“Unauthorized operation. Reclaiming control of the Forge.”_

**The Brassbound Forge-Tender** (CR ~3 adjusted for large party)

- **AC:** 17
    
- **HP:** 85
    
- **Speed:** 25 ft
    
- **Attacks:** 2× Hammer Slam (1d10+4 bludgeoning)
    
- **Heat Aura:** Any creature within 5 ft at end of turn takes 2 fire dmg.
    
- **Root Surge (Recharge 5–6):** The ground splits; brass-colored roots lash upward in a 15 ft radius. STR save DC 13 or be restrained until end of next turn.
    

**Environmental Hazards:**

- **Steam Vents (Lair Action, every round):** A random vent bursts, forcing a DEX save (DC 13) or take 2d6 fire.
    
- **Platform Tremor (Round 3):** Everyone must make a DEX save (DC 12) or fall prone as the bridge shakes.
    

---

### **Phase 2 — The Overheat**

When the Forge-Tender drops below 40 HP, it shouts:

> _“Pressure exceeding parameters. Initiating purge sequence.”_

Molten liquid begins spilling from broken pipes. The outer ring becomes hazardous terrain.

**Effects:**

- The outer ring now deals 2d8 fire dmg at the end of any turn spent there.
    
- Every round, a random **valve pipe bursts**, creating a temporary **geyser** (10 ft radius, DEX save DC 14, 3d6 fire dmg).
    

**New Ability — Molten Core Pulse (Recharge 5–6):**  
A wave of superheated air explodes outward. CON save DC 13, 2d8 fire dmg, half on success.

If a player uses one of the **valves** during this phase and succeeds on a STR (Athletics) DC 15 check, they can **vent pressure**, disabling Steam Vents for 1 round and weakening the Forge-Tender’s AC by -2 that round.

---

### **Phase 3 — Shutdown**

When the Forge-Tender is defeated:

> The automaton collapses to one knee, molten brass pouring from its joints like blood. It slams its hammer down beside the forge, stabilizing it. The room quiets as the pipes stop shaking, and the lava cools slightly. A faint green light flickers in its cracked chest cavity before dying out entirely.
> 
> The central forge hums — awaiting the final valve turn.

When the PCs activate the last valve, the **Flamegem** is automatically forged — a small, glowing red crystal that can be used to ignite the brazier in the main hall.

---

### **Loot**

- **Flamegem:** Used to light the left brazier. Emits faint warmth and glows when near heat sources.
    
- The Emberheart Mold
